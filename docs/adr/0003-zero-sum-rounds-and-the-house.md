# Rounds are zero-sum among players; forfeited pots go to a House that competes in the draw

The first build burned losers' Entries and paid winners a fixed operator-configured bonus, so total Entries drifted away from total Principal every round. The rebuild redistributes the whole round pot to the positions on the winning tile, as MinePEA does, so `Σ entries == total_principal + house.entries` at all times. When nobody covers the winning tile the pot is credited to the House, a Player account owned by the authority with zero Principal. The House therefore holds jackpot odds proportional to forfeited Entries, and when it wins, the jackpot splits 50% to a buyback reserve, 30% stays for the next epoch, 20% to treasury. This is the only revenue path in the product and it is intentionally weak: it exists to give the future token launch an auditable balance, not to fund operations. House Entries reset to zero every epoch so its odds cannot compound.
